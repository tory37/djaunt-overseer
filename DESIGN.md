# Design

The site (`docs/`) and the extension icon follow the Djaunt brand: https://github.com/tory37/djaunt-branding

- Theme: `hoard`. Tokens come from `docs/assets/tokens.css`, copied from `brand/tokens/tokens.css`. Use role variables only.
- Type: Archivo (display), IBM Plex Sans (body), IBM Plex Mono (labels, data, mock UI).
- Icons: Lucide at 1.5px stroke. No emoji.
- Geometry: 2/4/8px radii, 1px `--dj-border` hairlines, one `--dj-glow` per view (the download button).
